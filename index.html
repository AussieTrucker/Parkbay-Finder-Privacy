Privacy Policy - Park Bay Finder

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Park Bay Finder is an application designed to help truck drivers locate truck parking bays, rest areas and other useful stopping locations throughout Australia. This Privacy Policy explains what information Park Bay Finder uses, stores and shares.

1. Location Information

Park Bay Finder may request access to your device's location so that your current position can be displayed on the map and nearby locations can be identified.

Your normal location while using the map is not stored by Park Bay Finder or uploaded to the Park Bay Finder database.

If you choose to submit a missing park bay using your current location, the coordinates associated with that report may be submitted to the Park Bay Finder database for review.

2. User-Submitted Park Bay Reports

Users may voluntarily submit information about missing park bays or locations. A report may include information such as:

  • Location coordinates
  • Park bay or location name
  • Direction or side of the road
  • Road surface
  • Approximate truck or road-train capacity
  • Oversize vehicle suitability
  • Facilities such as toilets or bins
  • Additional notes supplied by the user

Submitted reports are transmitted to a Firebase/Firestore database and may be reviewed before being incorporated into Park Bay Finder's location data. Submitting a report is voluntary and does not require a Park Bay Finder account.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain information may be stored locally on your device, including favourite locations and acknowledgement of the application's safety notice.

This locally stored information is used to provide application functionality and is not uploaded to Park Bay Finder's database.

4. Analytics and Advertising

Park Bay Finder does not currently use advertising networks or analytics services to track users.

Park Bay Finder does not sell users' personal information.

5. Maps and Navigation

Park Bay Finder uses mapping functionality to display locations. When you choose to navigate to a location, Park Bay Finder may open Google Maps or another external mapping or navigation service installed on your device.

Use of an external mapping or navigation service is subject to that service provider's own privacy policy and terms.

6. Donations and External Services

Park Bay Finder may provide links to external services that allow users to voluntarily support the developer, such as Buy Me a Coffee or other payment services.

Payments are processed by the relevant external provider. Park Bay Finder does not directly collect or store payment card details.

7. Third-Party Services

Park Bay Finder may use third-party services necessary to provide application functionality, including Google mapping services and Google Firebase/Firestore. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

8. Sharing of Information

Park Bay Finder does not sell personal information to third parties. User-submitted park bay reports may be stored and reviewed for the purpose of maintaining and improving the application's location database.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information submitted through Park Bay Finder. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Park Bay Finder develops or as application features change. The latest version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests or other enquiries relating to Park Bay Finder, please contact the developer using the support email address listed on the Park Bay Finder Google Play Store listing.
